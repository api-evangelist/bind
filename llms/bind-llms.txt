# BindHQ

> BindHQ is a cloud-based policy administration and operations platform for specialty insurance. It unifies underwriting, policy management, billing, accounting, and reporting in one system for MGAs, MGUs, program administrators, and wholesale brokers.

BindHQ serves the specialty and excess & surplus (E&S) insurance market. The platform replaces fragmented legacy systems with a single integrated environment. It supports the full policy lifecycle from submission intake through accounting and compliance reporting. BindHQ offers API-first architecture for integrating with carriers, rating engines, data providers, and distribution partners.
